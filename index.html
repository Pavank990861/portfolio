03 / Learning reflection

Choosing the method
starts with the problem.

Comparing algorithms helps me separate three questions: how a model learns, what kind of data it works with, and what it is meant to accomplish. Supervised methods predict known targets, while unsupervised methods identify structure. Generative models introduce another goal: creating new content from learned patterns.

The algorithm framework connects these distinctions to my interests in AI and networking. Clustering can help explore groups in traffic measurements, while supervised methods can classify labeled events. The fruit-classification assignment demonstrates how to explain a complete decision process before implementation.

My next learning goal is to move from conceptual explanations to evaluated applications, with attention to data quality, privacy, and responsible use.
